Open Source Wizard

Hello, my name is Luke, and this is Open Source Wizard.

OSW is a personal blog where I document what I learn while working through software engineering concepts, systems, and projects. Writing things down helps me understand them more clearly, and this site exists as a record of that process over time.

The site is divided into two parts: Articles and Projects.

Articles focus on explanations of software engineering concepts, current ideas and trends, and structured tutorials.

Projects document things I have built or contributed to, including the decisions behind them, the issues I ran into, the tradeoffs I had to make, and what I learned along the way.

Although my main focus is embedded systems, I also write about networking, web development, and software engineering more broadly.

OSW is not a publishing platform for multiple authors. It is my own space for documenting work, sharing ideas, and building a body of writing that reflects my growth as an engineer.

The site itself is built with Flask and Python and is part of the project too.
